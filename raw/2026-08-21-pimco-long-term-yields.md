# What's Pushing Long-Term Bond Yields Higher?
# 机构: PIMCO (Marc Seidner, Pramol Dhawan) · 发布: 2026-08-21 · URL: https://www.pimco.com/us/en/insights/whats-pushing-long-term-bond-yields-higher
# 说明: WebFetch 结构化摘要 + 原文引用；非全文。

Three converging drivers have lifted 30-year Treasury yields to ~5.3%, a level unseen in nearly two decades: (1) sovereign debt — U.S. government debt surpassed $40 trillion, more than doubling in a decade; German and French 30-year yields at post-2011 / post-crisis highs; (2) a surge in longer-dated corporate bonds financing AI infrastructure, now large enough to compete with governments for capital, though not the dominant driver; (3) inflation concerns tied to energy costs — but breakevens stable, so the move is primarily a "real-yield event."
Other data: 10-year yield range 3.75%–4.75%; Japanese 10-year yields highest in three decades.
Implication: yields are an attractive entry point for long-term investors; compelling carry and roll-down on a steeper curve.
Quotes: "A confluence of rising sovereign debt, surging AI-related corporate bond issuance, and inflation concerns has lifted 30-year yields in the U.S. and elsewhere to two-decade highs." / "deficit reduction is the only durable anchor for long-end yields, in our view." / "current yield levels look increasingly appealing by historical standards, offering a compelling entry point for long-term investors."
