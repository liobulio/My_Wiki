# FERC Suspends PJM's Reliability Backstop Procurement Five Months; PJM Pulls Sept 30 Auction
Source: FERC order issued 2026-09-29 (eLibrary accession 20260929-3099), PJM Interconnection
Compiled from: FERC order + PJM statements, reported by Bloomberg, Utility Dive, EnergyConnects, RTO Insider, mgrid.org (2026-09-29/09-30)
URL (FERC eLibrary): https://elibrary.ferc.gov/eLibrary/filelist?accession_number=20260929-3099

---

## What happened (2026-09-29)

The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) issued an order **accepting** PJM
Interconnection's Reliability Backstop Procurement filing **but suspending it for five
months**, setting an effective date of **February 28, 2027**, subject to refund and to a
further "paper hearing" on three unresolved issues. In response, **PJM pulled its backstop
reliability auction that had been set to begin Wednesday, September 30, 2026**; a PJM
spokesman said "A new timeline for the backstop procurement is yet to be determined."

The backstop procurement was a one-time mechanism intended to acquire roughly **6,831 MW** of
new capacity to help close the reserve shortfall exposed by PJM's 2028/29 Base Residual
Auction (which cleared at the price cap with no new supply). It was designed as a reliability
"backstop" amid surging data-center load growth.

## The three issues FERC flagged as "may be unjust and unreasonable"

1. **Cost allocation** — FERC found PJM "did not adequately ensure that the procurement's
   costs will be allocated to the customers who drive its need," and proposed allocating costs
   on updated load forecasts instead (i.e., toward large new loads such as data centers, and
   away from residential consumers). This drew the sharpest comment.
2. **Transmission-owner exit rules** — the rules covering a transmission owner that exits PJM.
3. **Collateral requirements** for load-serving entities.

## Quotes

FERC Chair **Laura Swett**: "This commission will not be forced into accepting a deeply
flawed, eleventh-hour procurement mechanism with billion-dollar implications for consumers."

**TD Cowen** analyst John Miller (via Benzinga/Yahoo Finance, 2026-09-30): the decision
"further delays the procurement and creates 'renewed (and acute) uncertainty'" for the IPPs.

## Market reaction

Shares of **Constellation Energy (CEG)**, **NRG Energy (NRG)** and **Talen Energy (TLN)**
fell on the news (Yahoo Finance / Benzinga, 2026-09-30). Vistra (VST) was not named in the
stock-reaction headlines.

## Context — who proposed the mechanism

The Reliability Backstop Procurement traces to a **Joint Stakeholder Proposal** advanced by
**Constellation, Vistra, AlphaGen and Earthrise** (PJM RBPW, 2026-02-17) — i.e., the suspended
mechanism was one that CEG and VST had supported. Separately, PJM's rules for connecting large
loads and **co-located data centers** remain unsettled; PJM management has said it expects a
FERC order on co-location in Q1–Q2 2027 (a distinct proceeding from this backstop auction).
