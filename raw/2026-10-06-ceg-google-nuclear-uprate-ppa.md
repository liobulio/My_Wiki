Google and Constellation today announced a landmark long-term agreement to bring 890 MW of new, incremental nuclear capacity to the PJM Interconnection grid through uprates of existing Constellation-owned reactors, backed by a 20-year power purchase agreement (PPA) with Google.

## Key terms
- **890 MW of new nuclear capacity** via **uprates across 11 Constellation-owned nuclear units** in **Illinois, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey**. The upgrades increase thermal and electrical efficiency at existing facilities to unlock additional power for the grid.
- **20-year PPA** with Google underwrites the 890 MW of uprate capacity.
- Separate **15-year energy supply agreement** covering **2,700 MW of existing generation capacity** already operating in PJM.
- **More than $4.3 billion** of new investment by Constellation.
- Sustains approximately **4,400 existing jobs** and creates roughly **7,200 new construction jobs** during the building period.
- **First uprate (first capacity increment) expected by 2028.**
- **Electricity flows to the PJM Interconnection grid** (which serves ~67 million people) — grid-connected, NOT behind-the-meter / not co-located.
- **Five-year Google Cloud and Gemini Enterprise technology alliance** focused on three operational areas: accelerated permitting/interconnection planning, asset optimization and outage management, and critical infrastructure security.
- Companies also established a framework to evaluate future clean generation, storage and demand-response opportunities across the U.S., with initial focus on PJM.

## Quotes
- Amanda Peterson Corio, Google's global head of energy and power: "This long-term collaboration will strengthen the PJM grid while protecting energy affordability and supporting local union jobs."
- Joe Dominguez, Constellation CEO: "This collaboration can serve as a model for how technology companies and the energy industry can work together responsibly."
