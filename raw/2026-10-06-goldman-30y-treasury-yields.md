# Will 30-Year US Treasury Yields Climb Higher?
Source: Goldman Sachs Research (public insights article) · expert: Faisal Qubbaj (Goldman Sachs)
URL: https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/will-30-year-us-treasury-yields-climb-higher
Published: 2026-10-06

Key points (verbatim excerpts):

- "The Fed was very clear—it's not going to police the long bond," Qubbaj says. "Hiking the
  front end doesn't conjure up a bid for 30-year paper. The long end is going to have to find a
  yield that is attractive enough to bring in pensions and insurers. They are going to set the
  level, not the committee."

- Is US Treasury borrowing sustainable? "Deficits of roughly 6% to 7% of GDP at a time when
  nominal growth is coming in at roughly 6% mean the deterioration is closer to around 1% per
  year. That's a drift, not a spiral," Qubbaj says — the US can plausibly grow into its debt
  without severe austerity. "I don't think that's priced" into the Treasury market.
  "Sustainability and bond market clearing are different questions, and people conflate them."

- Are 30-year US Treasuries an investment opportunity? "30-year Treasury yields of 5.5% or
  higher are historically cheap, particularly in real, inflation-adjusted terms." But: "There
  is no clear catalyst" for 30-year Treasuries to rally amid heavy supply, uncertain inflation
  and cautious institutional buyers. With nominal growth ~5%, there is scope for the 30-year to
  rally toward 4.25%, but "yields are not likely to fall back to 3% anytime soon. To get
  something that low, you need a recession or a genuine growth shock, and that's not on my bingo
  card right now."
