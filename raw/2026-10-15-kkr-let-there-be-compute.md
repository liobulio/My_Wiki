## The Rush
Whether there is enough of the right capital, in the right form and at the right price, to finance the
ambition being priced today.

## The Field
Where the market interconnection actually resides, and why the diversification investors believe they own
may be diversification across wrappers rather than economic drivers.

## The Discipline
How underwriting discipline, thoughtful pacing, and portfolio construction can turn this rush into a
durable market as the trajectory evolves.

## Capital availability
"AI infrastructure capex through 2030 is estimated at ~$7.6-8 trillion." If the five largest hyperscalers
each reached roughly 3% weight in the investment-grade bond index, public bond markets could supply only
about $1.7 trillion in additional capacity. The remaining gap depends on internal cash flow and
alternative sources — private investment-grade offerings, asset-based finance, infrastructure debt,
structured solutions, and emerging financial structures — i.e. "how much of the buildout may be funded
outside the most obvious public debt and equity markets."

"AI-linked debt is already approximately $600 billion, or 6.3% of U.S. IG. The mix is broadening quickly:
AI-linked issuance was ~$189 billion in 2025 and has more than doubled year-over-year."

"Thirty-one issuers account for more than half a trillion dollars of bonds across the hyperscaler and AI
complex, and five names represent more than half of that total."

## Why it matters
The AI buildout will likely create opportunities across diverse financing markets, making capital form and
relative value increasingly important for investors evaluating compensation across the capital structure.
