# Constellation and Amazon Announce 20-Year Power Purchase Agreement Adding 190 Megawatts of Nuclear Capacity at Calvert Cliffs

Source: Constellation Energy newsroom (company press release) · Announced 2026-09-30
URL: https://www.constellationenergy.com/news/2026/09/constellation-and-amazon-announce-20-year-power-purchase-agreement-at-calvert-cliffs.html
(Corroborating coverage: ANS/Nuclear Newswire, ENR, NucNet, Fox Business, Maryland Matters, StockTitan CEG)

---

Constellation Energy and Amazon announced a **20-year power purchase agreement** supporting the
expansion of generating capacity at the **Calvert Cliffs Clean Energy Center** in Maryland.

Key terms:
- **690 megawatts** of power to Amazon under the 20-year PPA, **including a 190-megawatt uprate**
  (≈10% of the plant).
- Plant total capacity ~**1,790 MW** (two pressurized-water reactors).
- Enables **more than $3 billion in Maryland infrastructure investment**, including
  state-of-the-art improvements across the entire plant and ~190 MW of new, emissions-free
  generating capacity.
- **New capacity comes online between 2030 and 2032.**
- Power flows to the regional grid, **not behind-the-meter / not co-located**. Exact language:
  **"All the electricity generated by Calvert Cliffs will continue flowing to the PJM regional
  grid just as it does now."**
- Revenue certainty from Amazon helps **relicense the plant for another 20 years** (current unit
  licenses, renewed in 2000, expire in 2034 and 2036 unless subsequent renewal is pursued).

Quotes:
- **Joe Dominguez (Constellation CEO):** "This agreement demonstrates how private investment can
  strengthen critical energy infrastructure. Amazon's commitment supports the long-term operation
  of Calvert Cliffs for generations to come and creates a strong foundation for future investment
  in both the facility and in advanced nuclear technologies."
- **Kerry Person (AWS VP):** "Amazon is committed to investing in carbon-free energy to strengthen
  the grid and benefit the communities where we operate. This long-term agreement with
  Constellation sustains the continued operation and expansion of Maryland's largest source of
  carbon-free energy, supports hundreds of jobs, and brings significant new generation to the
  regional grid."
