Message: Fed Chair Kevin Warsh demonstrated more concise communication at his September press
conference — asking (fielding) fewer questions and giving shorter answers than at his June and
July appearances. Warsh is deliberately withholding forward guidance in the style of Paul
Volcker: he does not tell markets what the Fed will do at the next meeting, and lets the
decision itself carry the signal. He framed the September hike as removing "a dose of
accommodation" rather than as an outright tightening.

volatility elevated. Consistent with Apollo's standing higher-for-longer / "move up in quality"
