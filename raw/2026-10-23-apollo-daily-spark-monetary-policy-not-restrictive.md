Monetary Policy Is Not Restrictive

The Fed survey below for the third quarter of 2026 shows that only 19% of firms report
that financing costs or access to credit are constraining their investment and spending
plans, while 81% report no such constraints.

[Chart: Q3 2026 Fed survey — share of firms reporting financing costs / access to credit
as a constraint on investment and spending: 19% constrained vs 81% not constrained.]

(Torsten Slök's one-chart Daily Spark. The point: despite the policy rate level, corporate
financing conditions are not actually binding for the large majority of firms — i.e.
monetary policy is not restrictive — which argues against the premise that the Fed needs to
cut and is consistent with higher-for-longer.)
