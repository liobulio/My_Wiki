## What it's about (one paragraph, own words)
The paper treats a mean-reverting spread/price as an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU)
process and asks: given you're already in a position, what stop-loss,
take-profit, and maximum-holding-time levels maximize the trade's Sharpe
ratio? Prior work either relied on slow Monte Carlo simulation or assumed an
infinite (perpetual) trading horizon; this paper derives a fast,
semi-analytical solution (via the "method of heat potentials," borrowed from
heat-diffusion physics) that works for a realistic, finite horizon.
