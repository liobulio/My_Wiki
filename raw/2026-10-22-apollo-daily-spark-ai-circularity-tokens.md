# Circularity in AI Shows Up in Token Spending Too · Apollo (Torsten Sløk, The Daily Spark) · https://www.apollo.com/wealth/the-daily-spark/circularity-in-ai-shows-up-in-token-spending-too

Published: October 22, 2026

Ramp data show that 80% of the top 1% of AI spenders are VC-backed, compared with only 13% of the median spender, suggesting that the circularity seen among AI hyperscalers and model providers also extends to token spending, as the startups buying the most compute are paying for it with venture capital rather than organic earnings. If the AI payoff is slow to materialize or never arrives, VC returns would come under pressure, since the capital funding today's token spending would have little to show for it, and a pullback in venture funding could in turn weaken demand for compute.

Sources: Ramp AI Index, Apollo Chief Economist.
