# AI power demand

## Definition
The investment thesis that AI/data-center buildout is driving a structural, multi-decade rise
in electricity demand after two decades of flat US power demand — keeping power markets tight
and benefiting owners of existing generation (especially firm, carbon-free baseload).

## How it works
- Historically power demand grows ~with GDP, with spikes around power-intensive tech cycles.
- A data center ≈ "a refinery": electricity in → tokens/intelligence out (~$50B per GW).
- Supply is slow (10-yr build times, supply-chain/critical-mineral bottlenecks), so existing
  capacity re-rates. Hyperscalers lock long-term PPAs at premium fixed prices.
- Per the pitch, AI is not *required* for tightness — it "just turbocharges" an already-tight
  market.

## Pros / cons
- **Pro:** Long runway; firm baseload (nuclear/gas) is scarce and contractable.
- **Con / risks:** **Regulatory** — consumer power-bill backlash, price caps, nationalization
  risk; **rate sensitivity** — PPA cash flows discount with interest rates.
