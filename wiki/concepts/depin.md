# DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks)

## Definition
Crypto networks that use **token incentives** to bootstrap real-world physical infrastructure —
individuals buy and deploy hardware (sensors, base stations, antennas), provide a service, and
earn tokens. Lets a network scale faster and at lower capex than a centralized telecom.

## How it works
- Hardware owners earn tokens for supplying coverage/data; customers pay for the service.
- "Real-yield" models route a share of revenue to **buy back (and often burn)** the token,
  tying token value to usage. Watch the **mint vs burn** balance — many DePIN tokens stay
  net-inflationary for years.

## Pros / cons
- **Pro:** Capital-efficient, network-effect / natural-monopoly dynamics, fast buildout.
- **Con:** Many DePIN projects fail when token rewards lose value; revenue often small/early;
  tokens illiquid; securities-law uncertainty.
