# OpenAI (private)

## What it is
Frontier AI lab (ChatGPT, GPT-5.x). Now also designing **custom inference silicon** and a
much-anticipated **IPO candidate** (part of the ~$4T AI IPO backlog with [[anthropic]]).

## Current view
Vertically integrating into chips to cut inference cost and reduce [[nvda|Nvidia]] dependence,
while its huge compute deals ([[cerebras]]) ripple through the supply chain. Faces the same
**open-weight pressure** ([[ai-distillation-open-weights]], China's [[zai|GLM 5.2]]) as other
frontier labs. CFO **Sarah Friar** praised by [[bill-ackman]] for clear capital-commitment
framing ([[2026-06-03-ackman-market-is-missing]]).
