# CEG — Constellation Energy

## What it is
The largest US nuclear-fleet owner (spun out of Exelon in 2022), an independent power
producer selling into competitive markets, and the reference name for the
[[ai-power-demand]] trade. Known hyperscaler deals: the Microsoft PPA that restarts Three Mile
Island Unit 1 (Crane Clean Energy Center) and the Meta PPA at Clinton. ⚠️ Fleet size, the
Calpine acquisition status, and current contract terms are **to be verified from filings in
the first daily brief** — nothing here is fact-checked yet.

## Current view
Held by the human under [[ai-power-ipp-bull]]. From the Q2 2026 filings ([[2026-08-06-ceg-8k-q2-earnings]]): Crane
(TMI-1) restart cleared its two key regulatory gates (FERC CIR transfer waiver 2026-06; NRC fuel-license amendment), 920 MW of new
long-term nuclear PPAs signed, FY26 adjusted EPS guidance raised to $11.50–12.50, Calpine consolidated. ⚠️ PJM's $325/MW-day
capacity price cap now runs through 2029/30 (uncapped clearing would have been $554.72) — a policy ceiling on the upside.
Institutional backdrop: [[blackrock]] names power/grids as the AI bottleneck to own; [[goldman-sachs]] counts utilities among AI-capex
earnings beneficiaries. **Corporate development (2026-09):** agreed to acquire RISEC (609 MW combined-cycle gas, ISO-NE) from
Shell for $715M cash, close ~2027Q1 — adds merchant dispatchable capacity; management calls it immediately accretive at a
>10% unlevered return and no hit to the $5B buyback ([[2026-09-13-soochow-power-scarcity-nuclear-ppa]] lead, CEG press release).
**Hyperscaler PPA (2026-09-30):** signed a **20-year PPA with Amazon for 690 MW at Calvert Cliffs (incl. a 190 MW uprate)**,
backing **>$3B** of Maryland plant investment with ~190 MW of new carbon-free capacity online **2030–2032**; crucially the
power **continues flowing to the PJM grid (not co-located / not behind-the-meter)**, sidestepping the co-location/ISA controversy,
and the revenue certainty supports **relicensing the plant for another 20 years** (licenses expire 2034/2036). A textbook Catalyst
hit for [[ai-power-ipp-bull]] ([[2026-09-30-ceg-amazon-calvert-cliffs-ppa]], ✅ company press release).
**Hyperscaler PPA #2 (2026-10-06):** signed a **two-part long-term deal with Google** — a **20-year PPA** underwriting **890 MW of
new capacity from uprates across 11 Constellation-owned reactors** (IL/PA/NJ, **>$4.3B** investment, first increment by **2028**),
plus a **15-year supply agreement for 2,700 MW of existing PJM capacity**, and a 5-year Google Cloud/Gemini technology alliance.
Again the power **flows to the PJM grid (not co-located)**. Second hyperscaler long-term contract in a week — a direct Catalyst hit
for [[ai-power-ipp-bull]] ([[2026-10-06-ceg-google-nuclear-uprate-ppa]], ✅ Google/Constellation joint press release). CEG rose
~3–5% premarket. ⚠️ uprates still require NRC approval; PPA pricing undisclosed (data gaps).
