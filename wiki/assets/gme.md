# GME — GameStop

## What it is
Specialty retailer turned **collectibles** leader (trading cards/TCG, sports, pop-culture),
run by founder-operator [[ryan-cohen]]. Sits on a large cash pile and is using [[gme]] as an
acquisition platform — currently bidding for [[ebay]].

## Current view
Operationally transformed (cost-cut + collectibles pivot) with a fortress balance sheet, but
the stock carries a persistent **meme/"vibes" valuation** premium ([[bill-ackman]] cited it as
*the* example). Cohen's framing: short-term "voting machine," long-term "weighing machine" —
let the results compound. The [[ebay]] bid is the next big swing.
