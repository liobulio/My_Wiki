# MU — Micron Technology

## What it is
One of only **three** makers of AI-grade **HBM/DRAM** (with SK Hynix, Samsung) — the
[[hbm-dram-bottleneck|memory at the center of the AI bottleneck]]. The only US-based one.

## Current view
A structurally better memory cycle than past commodity-DRAM busts: 2026 HBM **sold out**,
take-or-pay floor-price contracts, pricing power. [[gavin-baker]]'s top "bottleneck" pick.
Risk: cyclicality, China **CXMT** flooding *consumer-grade* DRAM (not AI-grade), and the ~2-yr
lag to add capacity.

## ✅ Fact-check (Q3 FY2026, 2026-06-28)
- **Verified:** revenue ~quadrupled YoY; **FQ4 guide ~$50B ±$1B** (vs ~$44B est, ~$11B yr-ago);

  contracted revenue, take-or-pay floor pricing above prior-cycle peaks (covering ~20% of DRAM +
