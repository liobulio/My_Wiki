# BZ=F — Brent Crude Oil futures

## What it is
ICE Brent crude oil futures, the primary international/seaborne crude
benchmark (as opposed to [[cl|WTI]], the US-domestic benchmark). Continuous
front-month contract data via yfinance.

## Current view
Trading pair-mate to [[cl]] (WTI) in an active [[bz-cl-pairs-trade|statarb
research project]]. Brent is the benchmark most exposed to Persian Gulf
shipping risk — confirmed directly during the 2026 Iran–US Strait of Hormuz
conflict (started 2026-03-04): Brent spiked ~65% in March 2026 and moved
far more violently day-to-day than WTI on the same dates, consistent with
its greater exposure to that specific chokepoint. As of the last check, the
conflict's resolution was still uncertain (temporary ceasefire in early
April 2026; analysts discussing scenarios up to $200/barrel).

## Key data points
- 2026-03-04: Iran closes/attacks shipping through the Strait of Hormuz
  (~20% of global oil supply transits it)
- 2026-03: Brent > $114/barrel intraday; ~65% monthly rise, largest on record
- 2026-04-01: -14.5% single-day move as an early ceasefire eased prices
