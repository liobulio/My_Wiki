# TLN — Talen Energy

## What it is
US independent power producer (IPP) in the PJM grid. ~2.2 GW of **nuclear** (Susquehanna) plus
a natural-gas fleet. A primary beneficiary of surging [[ai-power-demand|data-center power
demand]] — has a major PPA(Power Purchase Agreement) with Amazon Web Services.

## Current view
A "buy a hard asset below replacement cost into a structural shortage" play
([[replacement-cost-investing]]). Besties ranked it **#2** (audience #1) — sizeable and
downside-protected, but more interest-rate-sensitive than [[mgm]] (PPA cash flows) and exposed
to AI/power **regulatory risk** (price caps, political backlash on consumer power bills).

## The pitch — [[dan-dreyfus]] (Bornite Capital)
- Power demand entering a multi-decade up-cycle; **AI just turbocharges** an already-tight market.
- Buy at ~$25B EV vs ~$45B replacement cost (>2x to equity just to reach replacement).
- Even doing nothing: ~$50/sh FCF (~7x; infra peers ~15x) → a double. More data-center
  contracts → ~$70/sh; build new capacity → $100+/sh.
