# Z.ai / Zhipu AI (private, China)

## What it is
Chinese AI lab (Zhipu AI) behind **GLM 5.2** — a frontier-class **open-weight** model under an
unrestricted **MIT license**. The poster child for China's open-source catch-up.

## Current view
GLM 5.2 is the strongest open-weight model to date and the clearest **evidence for the
[[ai-gatekeeping|fragmentation]] thesis** ([[david-friedberg]] over [[chamath-palihapitiya]]):
frontier-class coding at ~1/6 the cost, freely forkable. Trained on **Huawei** chips
(indigenization). Built-in censorship (Taiwan/Tiananmen) but US firms can fork & fix it.
