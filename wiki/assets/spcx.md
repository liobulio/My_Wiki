# SPCX — SpaceX

## What it is
Newly public space/launch + satellite-internet (Starlink) company led by [[elon-musk]]. Also
building launch vehicles, space-based broadband, and AI software. Per the source, just
completed the largest IPO ever and is acquiring the coding agent **Cursor**.

## Current view
A "one-of-one" mega-cap that IPO'd hot and, per Chamath, will "grow into" a rich valuation
rather than re-rate higher near-term (price/sales likely peaked at listing). Watch the
**Tesla/SpaceX merger** narrative and the Cursor/AI integration (note: SpaceX merged with xAI
earlier in 2026).

## ✅ Fact-check (2026-06-21)
- **Verified:** IPO **$135/sh**; **closed +19% to $161** on debut (June 12). IPO valuation
  **$1.77T**, pushed **>$2.6T by June 16** (episode's ">$2T" is right, if conservative);
  surpassed [[amzn|Amazon]] in market cap. — [CNBC IPO close](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html),
  [CNBC IPO explainer](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/09/spacex-ipo-explained-stock-price-date.html)
- **⚠️ Corrected:** raise was **~$75B** (555.6M sh × $135), not the episode's "$85B" — ~$10B
  overstated. Still ≈3× Saudi Aramco (2019). — [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/featured-articles/741/spacex-ipo)
- **Verified:** **Cursor** = ~$4B ARR (from ~$100M early 2025), acquired for **$60B all-stock**
  (~15× sales), closing Q3 pending regulatory. The April option was $60B-stock-or-$10B-breakup.
  — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/spacex-to-acquire-cursor-for-60b-in-stock-days-after-blockbuster-ipo/),
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/16/spacex-spcx-cursor-acquisition-ipo.html)
- **❓ Unverified (left as source-reported):** ~$19B 2025 revenue; "7th behind TSMC"; retail
  20–30% / 600–700k Robinhood allocations (IPO explainer says retail allocation was still TBD).
