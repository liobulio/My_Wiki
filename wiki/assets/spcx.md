# SPCX — SpaceX

## What it is
Newly public space/launch + satellite-internet (Starlink) company led by [[elon-musk]]. Also
building launch vehicles, space-based broadband, and AI software. Per the source, just
completed the largest IPO ever and is acquiring the coding agent **Cursor**.

## Current view
A "one-of-one" mega-cap that IPO'd hot and, per Chamath, will "grow into" a rich valuation
rather than re-rate higher near-term (price/sales likely peaked at listing). Watch the
**Tesla/SpaceX merger** narrative and the Cursor/AI integration. ⚠️ All figures below are
**source-reported (All-In, 2026-06-20) — verify**.
