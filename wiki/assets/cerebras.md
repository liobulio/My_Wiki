# CBRS — Cerebras Systems

## What it is
AI compute maker (wafer-scale chips) specializing in **inference / decode**; recent IPO. A case
study in **IPO pricing** and "**breaks deal price → forced selling**" dynamics ([[gavin-baker]]).

## Current view
Real demand (huge OpenAI contract) but the stock **broke its IPO price** after its first public
quarter when **margin guidance** (compressed by the OpenAI deal's costs) overshadowed the
backlog. Gavin's lens: ignore competitive noise; track **how fast they can bring on megawatts**
(power is the gating factor). Illustrates the broader IPO-absorption question for [[anthropic]]/
[[spcx]].
