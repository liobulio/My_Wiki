# MTUM — iShares MSCI USA Momentum Factor ETF

## What it is
A US large/mid-cap ETF that tracks an MSCI momentum index — the standard retail vehicle for
the [[momentum-factor]]. Rules-based: it reconstitutes semi-annually and can rebalance after
large volatility spikes.

## Current view
A clean, low-cost way to own US equity momentum. Because the wrapper is rules-based and
low-turnover, it's a "watered-down" version of academic momentum — expect a diversifying
return stream with periodic sharp drawdowns ([[momentum-crashes]]), not a smooth ride. Best
held alongside a value sleeve rather than standalone. See [[mtum-bull]].
