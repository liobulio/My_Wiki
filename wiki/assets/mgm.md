# MGM — MGM Resorts International

## What it is
US casino & resort operator: 13 Las Vegas properties (with Caesars, one of the two largest
Strip owners), plus MGM China (Macau) and a 40% stake in the upcoming **MGM Osaka** integrated
resort in Japan (opens 2030). Building a branded complex in Dubai.

## Current view
An **event-driven / sum-of-the-parts** situation. [[barry-diller]]'s People Inc. (formerly
IAC), already ~26% owner, has made a takeover bid — putting the company "in play." Bull case
rests on hidden Asian value (Osaka) and Dubai optionality the market under-prices. Besties
ranked it their **#1** pick: capped downside (the Diller bid as a floor) + real upside.

## The pitch — [[aaron-cowen]] (Suvretta Capital)
- Diller owns 26%, has bought aggressively; company also bought back ~half its float in 6 yrs.
- Vegas + China ≈ low-$60s; Osaka worth ~$50/sh; Dubai (if gambling legalizes) another $40–50.
- Diller is a **financial** buyer trying to get the asset cheap → don't tender; stock could be
  worth $100–150 (a "triple"). Catalyst: Diller showed his hand with the bid.
