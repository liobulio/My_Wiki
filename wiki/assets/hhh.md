# HHH — Howard Hughes Holdings

## What it is
Owner/developer of master-planned "small cities" (e.g. **Summerlin**, ~26,000 acres near Las
Vegas) — like the Irvine Company. Spun out of the General Growth Properties bankruptcy.
[[bill-ackman]]/Pershing Square (~47% owner) is converting it into a **Berkshire-style holding
company** via an [[insurance-float-compounding|insurance flywheel]].

## Current view
The actionable thesis from [[2026-06-03-ackman-market-is-missing]]: a real-estate compounder
the market has long ignored (trades at a discount to liquidation value, per Ackman ~$63/sh,
~$4B cap), now being **repurposed** — reinvest cash into insurance, invest the float, compound
for decades. Bet is on **management + the model**, not next-quarter real estate. Long-duration,
illiquid thesis; rate- and execution-sensitive. Related: [[replacement-cost-investing]] (buying
hard assets below their worth).
