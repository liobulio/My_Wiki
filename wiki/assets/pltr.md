# PLTR — Palantir Technologies

## What it is
Data/AI **application-layer** company (AIP, Foundry, Ontology, Apollo) serving governments and
enterprises. CEO [[alex-karp]]. Now a flagship **[[ai-sovereignty]]** vendor via its
[[nvda|Nvidia]] partnership.

## Current view
Structurally positioned to *win from a competitive model layer*: as an application/integration
player, Palantir wants **model optionality**, not dependence on one frontier lab — and sells
enterprises the ability to keep their data/weights. The **sovereign AI OS** (govt owns hardware,
data, weights) is a large TAM (McKinsey: ~$600B by 2030). Karp's anti-frontier-lab stance is
both marketing and moat.
