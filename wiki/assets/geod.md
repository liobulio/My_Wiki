# GEOD — GEODNET

## What it is
A crypto / [[depin|DePIN]] token for **GEODNET**, the world's largest decentralized **RTK**
(real-time kinematic) GNSS network — centimeter-level positioning (~2 cm vs GPS's ~1–2 m) for
robotics, drones, autonomous machines, and agriculture. Base stations are deployed by
individuals/businesses who earn GEOD tokens. Trades on **Solana**. CEO/founder **Mike Horton**.

## Current view
⚠️ **Real business, but the pitch's numbers are inflated.** A genuine network-effects /
"physical-AI" story with a working buyback-and-burn token model — but the headline revenue and
buyback figures are **~2x what reported data shows**, and the token is still **net-inflationary**
(more minted than burned) until ~2026–27. Besties ranked it **#4 / last**; too illiquid to size
(Chamath: couldn't deploy even $1M without moving the market) — a small, speculative "lottery
ticket."

## The pitch — [[kyle-samani]] (Multicoin Capital)
- ~22,000 base stations, 2x the legacy incumbents (Trimble/Hexagon/Topcon) combined; live in
  150 countries. Customers: John Deere, DJI, TomTom, robotic lawnmowers.
- ~**$11M** revenue, growing 3x YoY; **80%** of revenue (~$8.8M/yr) used to buy GEOD on the
  open market; ~$150M fully-diluted valuation.

## ⚠️ Fact-check (2026-06-19)
- **Overstated:** Reported revenue is **~$5M annualized** (Q3 2025 ≈ $1.2M/qtr, +217% YoY),
  **not ~$11M**. So buybacks ≈ 80% × ~$5M ≈ ~$4M/yr, not $8.8M. Samani's own transcript even
  contradicts itself ("$1M run rate" then "$11M"). — [Messari Q3 2025](https://messari.io/report/state-of-geodnet-q3-2025),
  [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/protocol/geodnet)
- **Verified:** ~**20,500+** base stations; largest/fastest-growing RTK network. — [Messari](https://messari.io/report/state-of-geodnet-q3-2025)
- **Verified:** 80% of data revenue → **buyback-and-burn**; migrating to **Solana** (GIP7);
  CEO **Mike Horton** (also co-founder/CTO Anello Photonics). — [Mike Horton / X](https://x.com/mikeahorton/status/2007509806517801212)
- **Verified + conflict:** Multicoin **led an $8M round (Feb 2025)**; total ~$15M raised —
  Samani disclosed he owns the token, not equity. — [Inside GNSS](https://insidegnss.com/geodnet-raises-8m-to-equip-humanoid-robots-with-centimeter-level-accuracy/)
- **Nuance the pitch glossed:** "net-positive" (burn > mint) only **expected 2026–27** — until
  then token supply is still inflating.

## Key data points
- ~20,500 stations; revenue ~$5M annualized (not $11M); 80% buyback-burn; Solana; CEO M. Horton.
- Highest risk + lowest liquidity of the four picks.
