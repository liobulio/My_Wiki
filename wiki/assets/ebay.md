# EBAY — eBay

## What it is
Global online **marketplace** (no first-party inventory). Strong in niche/secondary categories
(collectibles, refurbished tech, used parts) where Amazon is weak. The **target of [[ryan-cohen]]
/ [[gme|GameStop]]'s $56B bid**.

## Current view
Per [[2026-06-24-ryan-cohen-ebay-gamestop]]: a **great asset, poorly run** — durable marketplace
moat but stagnant since COVID, opex >half of revenue, sellers (the real customers) alienated,
share lost to Shopify/social/live commerce. Cohen's bull-if-acquired case: cut $2B costs, fix
**eBay Live** (live commerce, ~$400B TAM), and build an **in-game digital collectibles**
marketplace. **Board rejected** the bid — so the catalyst is a contested M&A/proxy fight, not
operations.

## ✅ Fact-check (2026-06-26)
- **Verified:** GameStop bid **$56B / $125/sh** (50% cash, 50% stock); eBay board **rejected
  May 12, 2026** as "neither credible nor attractive" (financing/governance concerns). —
  [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/ebay-rejects-gamestop-s-56-billion-takeover-as-not-credible),
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/12/ebay-rejects-gamestops-takeover-ryan-cohen.html)
- **✅ Historical:** divested PayPal, Skype (→ MSFT 2011), StubHub (→ Viagogo ~$4B); Donahoe left ~2015.
- **❓ Source-reported (Cohen's framing):** "active users −30M since COVID," opex >half revenue,
  and the entire three-part turnaround plan (his *projection*).
