# PSUS — Pershing Square USA

## What it is
[[bill-ackman]]'s US-listed **closed-end fund** — a way to own Pershing Square's "best ideas"
in one ticker. Permanent capital; 2% management fee. (Sister vehicles: **Pershing Square Inc.**,
the now-public *manager*; and **PSH**, the London-listed fund.)

## Current view
A **discount-to-NAV** play: you buy Pershing's portfolio below its net asset value. Ackman
pitched it at an "18% discount," but the structural reality of his closed-end funds is
**persistent, often-widening discounts** — so the discount is both the appeal (entry) and the
risk (it may not close).
