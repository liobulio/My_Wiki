# AKTS — Aktis Oncology

## What it is
Clinical-stage biotech building an **actinium-225 alpha-emitter** [[radiopharmaceuticals|
radiopharmaceutical]] **miniprotein** platform for solid tumors. Lead program **AKY-1189**
targets **nectin-4** (bladder/urothelial + other tumors); second program targets **B7-H3**.
First biotech IPO of 2026; Eli Lilly–backed.

## Current view
A platform-optionality biotech bet: de-risked by validated targets, imaging-confirmable target
engagement, a scarce actinium-225 supply chain (a real moat, hard for China to replicate), and
strong pharma M&A appetite in radiopharma. Besties ranked it **#3** — a "lottery ticket": 10x
if a program works, meaningful chance of a large drawdown on a bad readout.

## The pitch — [[oleg-nodelman]] (EcoR1 Capital)
- "Monetize other people's science"; margin-of-safety, not science-worship.
- ~$1B mkt cap, ~$500M EV, 3+ yrs cash past key milestones; data in 2027 (nectin-4 as early as
  Q1). Could be worth ~$10B / ~$200/sh if even one program reaches market.
