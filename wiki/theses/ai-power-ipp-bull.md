**The Edge** — All three are heavy-asset, nuclear-heavy independent power producers. A
comparable fleet cannot be rebuilt in less than 7–10 years, so supply is fixed on any
investable horizon while power demand rises. Buy the assets **below replacement cost**
([[replacement-cost-investing]]). Even after the contracts already signed, each still has
ample uncontracted dispatchable power to sell into a tightening market.

**The Catalyst** — New long-term power contracts with hyperscalers (**AWS, Microsoft**, and
peers): each signing re-rates the remaining uncontracted capacity.

**The Invalidation** — condition-based (the human set no price stop). Any one hit triggers a
review; the position page tracks each as `ok | watch | hit`:
1. Hyperscaler power contracts are **delayed or cancelled**.
2. Nuclear **uprate / co-location agreements are restricted by regulators** (FERC, NRC, state
   PUCs) — e.g. FERC's 2024-11-01 rejection of the amended Susquehanna–AWS ISA.
3. **Solar capacity expansion runs faster than expected**, eroding the scarcity of dispatchable
   power.
4. **Solar plant rebuild cost falls**, undercutting the replacement-cost anchor.

## Thesis
Data-center load growth has turned US power from a flat market into a structural shortage of
firm, carbon-free capacity. Existing nuclear plants are the only large source of 24/7 clean
power available this decade; their owners ([[ceg]], [[tln]], [[vst]]) can sign hyperscaler
contracts at premiums to wholesale while the fleet trades below what it would cost to build.
See [[ai-power-demand]].

## Risks / counter-thesis
- ⚠️ **Anchor risk (2026-10-06, [[2026-10-06-ceg-vst-tln-replacement-cost-summary]]):** the Edge holds on
  new-build anchors (Q_B ≈ 0.46–0.52) but not on private-deal anchors (Q_D ≈ 1.6–1.8); at the
  conservative new-build anchor A, VST/TLN are already near Q=1 (+8–9%), CEG +26%. Weakest link,
  **not covered by the four Invalidations:** nuclear's modern-equivalent asset may be a CCGT —
  if buyers stop paying a carbon-free/24×7 premium (AWS ~$88/MWh vs PTC floor $44.75), the
  discount disappears. See [[ceg-vst-replacement-cost-2026-10-06]].
- Regulatory: FERC co-location rulings, PJM capacity-market interventions, political backlash
  on retail power bills ([[tln]] pitch caveats).
- ⚠️ **Midterm-election policy risk (new 2026-10-09)** — Invalidation #1 上游。两家内资独立
  提出（[[2026-10-09-soochow-us-midterm-elections|东吴]]、[[2026-10-09-cpost-overseas-macro-yields-tech|中邮]]）：
  2026-11-03 中期选举若使左翼民主党扩大国会席位，AI 基础设施监管或收紧——要求科技企业
  承担数据中心扩张的电网升级/电力/环境外部成本（推升单位 capex、约束 AI CAPEX），甚至
  限制前沿模型训练/数据中心扩张速率。催化有明确日期、方向负面、强度未知；目前无电力合约
  推迟/取消实证，故属 Invalidation #1 的 watch 观察项，尚未触发。
  **外资独立印证（2026-10-12 补）**：两家外资就同一中选政策风险独立发声——
  [[2026-10-10-morgan-stanley-midterm-market-impact|Morgan Stanley]]"若民主党在国会取得更多
  控制，**科技公司与能源生产商可能面临更强政治审视**"（明确点名 energy producers）、
  [[2026-10-06-pimco-cyclical-outlook-growth-holds|PIMCO]]"在 **AI 相关信用上须为监管/法律风险
  与美国中选政治寻求更高风险补偿**"（信用定价角度）。至此四家（东吴/中邮 + 外资 MS/PIMCO）
  独立提出，风险信号跨内外资、跨股债。**缓冲侧**：MS 同判"**分裂政府限制系统性政策变动、降低
  市场扰动风险**"、PIMCO 判"近期政策增强央行可信度"——维持 watch、非 hit。
  **内资深度扩充至六家（2026-10-16 补）**：临近 11-03，两家内资独立出中选专题——
  [[2026-10-16-guojin-midterm-war-fiscal-ai|国金]]"**国会影响预期、地方影响现实**"：联邦层面即便
  民主党横扫也难立法限 AI（特朗普否决权），但**地方州长/州议会选举把对数据中心建设的约束变成现实**
  （两党选民皆对 DC 建设负面；切入点＝要求开发商承担更多公用事业成本、降低居民电费、提高环境标准、
  取消税收优惠）——据 Distilled，**10 个竞争州共涉 67.4 GW 计划数据中心产能（与美国当前已运营 DC 容量
  相当）**；[[2026-10-16-cmbc-intl-midterm-guide|民银国际]]"民主党参众两院可**对 AI 模型与算力资本开支
  增加监管约束**、强化科技反垄断，**能源板块承压**（限油气补贴/收紧审批资金）"。至此东吴/中邮 + 外资
