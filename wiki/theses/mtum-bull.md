# MTUM — bull (watchlist) thesis

> **Watchlist thesis** — drafted from the sample source. Not a real position. Conviction is
> tentative (2/5) pending the human's own view and verified data.

## Thesis
US equity [[momentum-factor|momentum]], owned via [[mtum]], is a real and persistent return
premium that **diversifies** a value-tilted book. Hold it as a sleeve sized for its crash
risk, not as a standalone bet.

## Supporting evidence
- Momentum is robust across asset classes and persistent over decades. — [[2026-06-12-quant-corner-momentum]]
- Negatively correlated with value → improves combined risk-adjusted returns. — [[2026-06-12-quant-corner-momentum]]
- [[mtum]] gives cheap, rules-based access. — [[2026-06-12-quant-corner-momentum]]

## Risks / counter-thesis
- **[[momentum-crashes]]:** fat left tail in sharp post-crash rebounds (e.g. 2009).
- Long flat stretches — can underperform for years.
- ETF wrapper dilutes the academic premium (low turnover, infrequent rebalance).

## Falsifiers (what would invalidate it)
- Momentum delivering negative risk-adjusted returns over a full cycle vs a value-only book.
- MTUM tracking error / methodology change that breaks the factor exposure.
