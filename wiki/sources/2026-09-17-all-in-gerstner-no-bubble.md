# Brad Gerstner — "No AI Bubble, Semis Eat the Nasdaq"

> [[all-in-podcast]] keynote by guest [[brad-gerstner]] (Altimeter). Partial machine transcript
> (~16.6k chars via podscripts.co; ASR garbles names). Recorded at the All-In Summit **before**
> the 9/16 FOMC ("I think rate hikes are coming… over 90% chance tomorrow… we'll hear more
> tomorrow"), published 2026-09-17.

## TL;DR
Altimeter 的 Gerstner 用一组图给出"这不是 2000 年泡沫"的多头框架：AI 行情由**盈利驱动**（EPS +26%）而非估值扩张，
[[nvda]] 仅 ~14× 明年完税 GAAP 盈利、NASDAQ/S&P/SOX 均**低于**历史均值倍数；半导体贡献了 **NASDAQ 涨幅的 70%**。
但他把叙事从"要不要上车 AI"切换到"**要相信什么才能让行情延续**"——核心是 AI 实验室的**月度营收**能否从 ~$100B run-rate
翻到年底 ~$180B，以支撑 ~$1.5T/年的 capex。三大风险：监管、**电力**、利率——其中"**电力设备已售罄**、电网互联延迟、
许可与地方阻力"使他判断 2027 年 43 GW 的算力扩张**太激进**、实际约 25 GW，直接印证 [[ai-power-ipp-bull]] 的 firm 电力稀缺。

## Key takeaways
- **"Not 2000."** 行情是 earnings-driven：EPS **+26% YTD**，NASDAQ/S&P **倍数反而收缩**；[[nvda]] ~14× 明年完税 GAAP。
  "This is no bubble like it was in 2000." NASDAQ/S&P/SOX/NVIDIA 均低于历史均值倍数，Mag 7 大致等于均值。
- **半导体吃掉纳指。** "Semiconductors are 70% of the NASDAQ return." 卖铲子的（semis）赚钱，买 token 的搭便车；
  hyperscaler capex ≈ 逐美元流向 semis 的自由现金流；Dell +5×、[[skhynix|Hynix]] +9×（18 个月）。
- **The offtake-revenue test（他最看重的单一指标）。** 顶级实验室（Anthropic/OpenAI/xAI）合计 run-rate ~$100B，
  需年底到 ~$180B（+$80B）才能"keep the AI trade intact"；capex $1.5T/年，offtake 需从 ~$200B → $450B → $800B–$1T。
  TAM 不是问题——只要拿到知识工作 TAM 的 ~4%（~$1.2T）即可覆盖 capex。
- **AI take-off / 三种情景。** 若实验室月营收接近 **$8B**→"takeoff"、年内或见 IPO；若 ~$4B 则横盘。
  今年产出 **47 quadrillion tokens**、Codex 用户 8 个月 +40×、企业 AI 支出 18 个月 +17×。
- **三大风险：监管 · 电力 · 利率。**
  - 电力："our total compute in the country is **less than 40 GW**"；2026 新增 ~19 GW（~7 GW 给头部实验室），
    SemiAnalysis(Dylan Patel) 预测 2027 +43 GW（14 GW 给实验室）；Gerstner 认为**太激进**，实际 ~25 GW——
    "**power equipment is sold out**", grid-interconnection delays, permitting/local opposition, skilled-labor shortages。
    并抨击历史上关停 67 座裂变反应堆是"灾难"，警告勿对 AI 重演过度监管。
  - 利率："over 90% chance of rate hikes tomorrow"；借钱建数据中心→hurdle rate 上升；"interest rates are to stocks
    what gravity is to matter"；若 10Y 到 **5.5%** 对股市是重负。
  - 监管：Anthropic IPO 若被暂停/推迟是重大风险（他认为不会发生）。
- **仓位：medium, mentally flexible.** "Everybody knows about AI. It's all priced. Now it's about facts and
  circumstances… Don't Yolo. Don't go 4X."（暗指北方某人 4× 杠杆全押 Citadel。）

## Notable claims
- "Look at Nvidia, trading at **14 times** next year's fully taxed gap earnings. This is no bubble like it was in 2000."
- "**Semiconductors are 70% of the NASDAX return.** … The makers of the tokens are making the money, and the buyers of the tokens are basically going along for the ride."
- "I think you have to go from **200 to 450 to 800 or a trillion** dollars just in keep up… otherwise we can't build this much cap X."
- "Our total compute in the country is **less than 40 gigawatts**. Doing this in one year… permitting and local opposition… grid interconnection delays… **power equipment is sold out**. I would suggest Dillon's forecast of **43 gigawatts** next year is too aggressive… closer to **25 gigawatts**."
- "By 2028… over **half of the total compute** in the country is controlled by two labs."
- "**Interest rates are to stocks what gravity is to matter**… if rates were to go to five and a half on the 10 year, that's going to be a big burden on the equity market."

## Agrees / contradicts
- **Agrees with [[ai-power-ipp-bull]] / [[ai-infra-power-grid-bottlenecks-2026-09]]:** "power equipment is sold out",
  grid-interconnection delays and permitting make even the leading labs' build "too aggressive" — a market-side
  confirmation of firm-power scarcity, from a large AI-long fund. Echoes [[green-dahua-futures]]'s "每 GW" framing
  ([[2026-09-18-greendh-futures-ai-reaccel]]) and [[guojin-securities]]'s "电力约束是行业 Beta"
  ([[2026-09-13-guojin-compute-mainline-power]]).
- **Agrees with the domestic rate-risk camp:** rate hikes + "10Y 到 5.5% 对股市是重负" lines up with
  [[dongxing-securities]] 维持 10Y 4.7–5% ([[2026-09-18-dongxing-fomc-oil]]) and the [[soochow-securities]]/
  [[donghai-securities]] rate-sensitivity flag — Gerstner treats rates as a real headwind, not just noise.
- **⚠️ Tensions with the AI-bubble/ROI skeptics:** directly rebuts the "2000 redux" fear ([[nvda]] 14× vs 2000);
  but concedes the whole trade is now hostage to lab **offtake revenue** — a sharper version of Apollo's
  "can AI earn it back?" ([[2026-09-11-apollo-daily-spark-ai-roi]]). His caveat "it's all priced… facts &
  circumstances" is a de-facto answer to [[guosen]]'s "AI 牛市 vs 高利率" question ([[2026-09-18-guosen-us-bull-bears]]):
  no longer one-decision beta, now stock-picking in a rate-sensitive tape.

## Fact-check / Data gaps
- ✅ **[[nvda]] ~14× fwd fully-taxed GAAP · "not 2000" · semis 70% of NASDAQ return** — corroborated as Gerstner's
  own All-In Summit framing (FourWeekMBA write-up of the keynote; consistent with NVDA fwd-P/E range). His analysis, not third-party fact.
- ✅ **"Two labs control >half of US compute by 2028"** — a documented SemiAnalysis/Dylan Patel projection
  (Dealroom, 404kresearch). Scenario, not fact.
- ⚠️ **SemiAnalysis 2027 GW figures** — Gerstner cites **43 GW total / 14 GW to labs**; public SemiAnalysis prints run
  **~50 GW total additions / ~18 GW frontier-lab** for 2027. Same ballpark, numbers differ — treat as **projection**,
  not verified fact. His own "~25 GW actual" and "<40 GW total US compute" are estimates/opinions.
- **Projections (not fact-checkable, assessed):** lab run-rate $100B→$180B by year-end, offtake $200B→$1T, "$8B/mo =
  takeoff", 47 quadrillion tokens, Codex +40×, enterprise AI +17×, Dell +5× / Hynix +9× — the last two are checkable
  price moves but unverified here; flagged as data gaps.
