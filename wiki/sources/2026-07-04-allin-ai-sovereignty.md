# All-In ep. 279 — AI Sovereignty Wars

The besties (no guests) on the **[[ai-sovereignty]]** theme (Palantir-Nvidia, Karp vs the
frontier labs), the AI-jobs data, Anthropic's Fable un-ban, and California's budget.

## TL;DR
The episode's thesis: enterprises should **own their compute/models/data/"alpha"** rather than
feed proprietary IP to frontier labs that vertically integrate against them. **[[pltr|Palantir]]
+ [[nvda|Nvidia]]** launched a **sovereign AI** engine (Nemotron open models, air-gapped) — more
[[ai-gatekeeping|fragmentation]] evidence. **[[anthropic]]'s** Fable ban was **lifted**. Jobs
data shows **displacement, not loss**. California's "balanced budget" masks structural deficits.

## 1. AI sovereignty / Palantir-Nvidia / Karp `[00:21]`
- **[[pltr]] + [[nvda]] "sovereign AI OS":** Palantir deploys Nvidia **Nemotron open models** in
  classified/air-gapped govt environments; agencies own hardware, data, **model weights**.
- **[[alex-karp]]** (CNBC) rips frontier labs: enterprises want control of "compute, models, data
  stack, and **alpha**" — don't hand your IP to a provider that will compete with you. →
  [[ai-sovereignty]].
- **[[fig|Figma]] cautionary tale:** [[anthropic]] launched **Claude Design** (its CPO sat on
  Figma's board, resigned 3 days pre-launch); Figma ~−50% YTD. Anthropic also shipped Claude
  Code/Science/Security/Legal/Financial — the **Microsoft/Google "dominate the OS, capture the
  verticals"** playbook.
- **Roll-your-own:** [[chamath-palihapitiya]] (8090) — their harness on an open model ran
  **16.4× cheaper** than Opus 4.8 alone (3× slower); host [[zai|GLM]] on your own US hardware.
  [[david-friedberg]] (Ohalo) — life-sciences firms refusing Anthropic's data-sharing; "large
  hub → medium hub → **distributed spoke**." [[david-sacks]]: model layer is an **Anthropic/OpenAI
  duopoly** (~$60B / ~$40B ARR); keep it **competitive**; token costs to fall ~90%/yr for 3 yrs.

## 2. AI jobs debate `[33:52]` — displacement ≠ loss
- **RAMP/Revelio study** (21,559 firms): heavy AI adopters grew headcount **+10.2%** (entry-level
  **+12%**) — no present-day job-loss signal. **[[ai-jobs-displacement]]**.
- Calacanis: displacement *is* coming (customer support, data entry, Waymo/Optimus drivers).
  **Klarna reversed** its AI-customer-service replacement → "**human in the loop**" premium.

## 3. Anthropic Fable 5 restored `[50:24]`
- Commerce Sec **Lutnick lifted** export controls (~June 30); **Fable 5 back July 1**, Mythos
  restored. Anthropic agreed to proactively detect risks + work on standards. Co-founder **Tom
  Brown replaced [[dario-amodei]]** as lead negotiator. Postmortem: jailbreak "not unique."
- Imports debate: [[david-sacks]] — a Chinese open model "**stops being Chinese**" once forked
  and run on US hardware; banning open-source would isolate the US / impose a "token tax."

## 4. SCOTUS birthright citizenship `[59:06]` (political — brief)
Court struck down Trump's EO ending birthright citizenship (6-3). **Not investment-actionable.**

## 5. Newsom's CA budget `[1:21:30]` — [[california-fiscal-risk]]
Newsom signed a "$351B balanced budget" ($0 deficit through 2028), but critics (LAO/CalMatters)
say it **masks $20–35B/yr out-year deficits** via borrowing. Tax-base concentration (top 1% pay
~half of income tax), corporate/high-earner **exodus** to TX/FL, huge pension liabilities, new
software/health taxes, a proposed **billionaire tax**. Friedberg: default/bailout tail risk.
