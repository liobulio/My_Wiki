# Ryan Cohen — the $56B eBay plan (All-In interview)

[[david-friedberg]] 1-on-1 with [[ryan-cohen]] (chairman/CEO of [[gme|GameStop]]) on his
hostile-ish bid for [[ebay]]. A clinic in **founder-operator** thinking ([[founder-led-companies]]).

## TL;DR
Cohen — who built [[chewy]] and turned [[gme|GameStop]] around — is bidding **$56B ($125/sh,
50% cash/50% stock)** for [[ebay]], arguing it's a great asset run badly. eBay's board
**rejected** it. His edge: he's a [[founder-led-companies|founder-operator]] putting his own
money in; their management has "no skin in the game."

## Key takeaways
- `[01:56]` **Chewy:** built pet e-comm vs Amazon on "a game of pennies" (negative working
  capital, supply-chain obsession, "will over skill" hiring); sold **2017 for $3.35B**.
- `[11:58]` **GameStop:** started passive (<5%, 2020), crossed 5% → filed **13D** (activist),
  joined board 2021 (meme squeeze; co. raised ~$1.7B, wiped debt). Became CEO → maniacal
  cost-cutting, pivoted to **collectibles** (now ~42% of revenue). See [[activist-investing]].
- `[26:39]` **Why [[ebay]]:** complementary to GameStop's collectibles/secondary-market/
  authentication; global scale; within his "circle of competence" (e-commerce). eBay's been
  mismanaged since COVID — every key metric down, opex >half of revenue, **sellers alienated**
  (sellers are the real customer).
- `[43:58]` **Three-part plan:** (1) cut **$2B** of costs (from ~$5.5B opex; $2.4B S&M for no
  user growth); (2) **live commerce** (eBay Live; TAM ~$400B; use 1,600 stores as creator
  studios/fulfillment); (3) a **digital / in-game collectibles** marketplace ("what NFTs should
  have been, but with real utility").
- `[49:33]` eBay **rejected** the bid (financing uncertainty, governance); minimal engagement.
  Failed vote to lower special-meeting threshold 20%→10%. Cohen: "I'm not going to go away."

## Notable claims (checked below)
- [[gme]] Q1: collectibles ~42% of revenue ($350M); revenue $835M (+14% YoY); SG&A 228→202;
  $9.7B cash; $333M FCF; buyback authorized.
- [[ebay]] bid $56B, 50/50 cash/stock at a premium; Cohen putting **$500M of his own money** in.
- [[ebay]] since COVID: GMV down, active users down ~30M, revenue ~breakeven, opex up a lot.

  41.8%** (+65% YoY); cash **$9.7B**; **$2B buyback**; record net income $389.6M / op income
  $143.3M. — [TradingView/10-Q](https://www.tradingview.com/news/tradingview:ed89ded4652bf:0-gamestop-corp-1q-2026-revenue-835-3m-net-income-389-6m-eps-0-66-10-q-summary/),
