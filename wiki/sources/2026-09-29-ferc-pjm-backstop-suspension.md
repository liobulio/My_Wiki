# FERC suspends PJM's Reliability Backstop Procurement (2026-09-29)

> Regulatory order — chased from a news lead (Yahoo/Benzinga "CEG, NRG, TLN Stocks In
> Spotlight… FERC Delays PJM's Plan") into the primary FERC order. No [[ceg]]/[[tln]]/[[vst]]
> 8-K was filed on it (`sec_fetch.py --since 2026-09-28` → CEG 0 / TLN 1 unrelated capacity-
> monetization 8-K / VST 0), so the citation is the FERC order itself.

## TL;DR
On **2026-09-29** FERC **accepted but suspended** PJM's one-time Reliability Backstop
Procurement for **five months** (effective **2027-02-28**, subject to refund + a paper
hearing), and PJM **pulled its Sept 30 backstop auction** (~**6,831 MW** target to cover the
2028/29 reserve shortfall). FERC flagged three parts as possibly "unjust and unreasonable":
cost allocation, transmission-owner exit rules, LSE collateral. Shares of [[ceg]], NRG and
[[tln]] fell. It is a **PJM capacity-market intervention** in the Invalidation #2 channel of
[[ai-power-ipp-bull]], but it does **not** restrict any of our companies' own uprate/
co-location agreements — the mechanism suspended was one [[ceg]]/[[vst]] had co-proposed.

## Key takeaways
- **Accept-but-suspend**, not reject: effective date pushed to **2027-02-28**, subject to
  refund and a paper hearing on three issues.
- **PJM pulled the Sept 30 auction**; new timeline "yet to be determined."
- **Cost allocation** was the sharpest finding: FERC said PJM "did not adequately ensure that
  the procurement's costs will be allocated to the customers who drive its need" and proposed
  allocating on updated load forecasts (toward large new loads / data centers).
- The other two flagged issues: **transmission-owner exit rules** and **LSE collateral**.
- **TD Cowen** (John Miller): "renewed (and acute) uncertainty" for the IPPs — a news read,
  not a tracked-institution page.

## Agrees / contradicts
- **Reinforces** the thesis's listed Regulatory risk ("PJM capacity-market interventions") and
  the firm-power scarcity story in one sense: suspending procurement of **new** capacity leaves
  the 2028/29 reserve shortfall unfilled longer → tighter market for existing dispatchable
  fleets. But it **delays** the price/contracting signal and adds regulatory uncertainty
  (stocks fell; TD Cowen "acute").
- ⚠️ Touches the **Invalidation #2 channel** (FERC / PJM capacity-market intervention) of
  [[ai-power-ipp-bull]] — see Thesis check. Distinct from the still-pending **co-location**
  rulemaking (FERC order expected Q1–Q2 2027), which is the proceeding that most directly
  governs [[ceg]]/[[tln]] monetizing uncontracted capacity.

## Thesis check — [[ai-power-ipp-bull]]
The four Invalidation conditions: (1) hyperscaler contracts delayed/cancelled · (2) uprate/
co-location agreements **restricted by regulators (FERC/NRC/PUC)** · (3) solar expansion faster
than expected · (4) solar rebuild cost falls.

- **Condition #2 (FERC/PJM regulatory restriction)** — this is a FERC suspension of a PJM
  capacity-market *mechanism*, explicitly in the channel the condition names. It does **not**
  restrict any specific CEG/TLN/VST **uprate or co-location agreement**, so it is **not a hit**;
  it is the most concrete FERC/PJM-channel development since the thesis opened, so → **watch**
  for the PJM-exposed names **[[ceg]]** and **[[tln]]** (shares fell; "acute uncertainty").
- **[[vst]]** — ERCOT/Texas-centric, was a **co-proposer** of the mechanism and not named in the
  stock-reaction; its firm-power exposure (Odessa behind-the-meter, [[2026-09-21-newera-8k-vistra-luminant-ppa]])
  is not PJM-backstop-dependent → **ok** (unchanged).
- Conditions #1, #3, #4 — untouched.
