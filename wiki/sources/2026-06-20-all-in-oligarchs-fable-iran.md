## 2. SpaceX IPO + Cursor + trillionaire `[14:27]`
- **[[spcx|SpaceX]] IPO** (~June 11–12, 2026): priced **$135/sh**, filled the green shoe for an
  **~$85B raise** (≈3× Saudi Aramco 2019); closed **+19% to $161**, **cap >$2T**; ~**$177** at
  taping. Briefly 4th-most-valuable (passed AMZN, MSFT), then ~7th (behind TSMC). 2025 revenue
  ~$19B. ~20–30% of the IPO allocated to **retail** (~600–700k Robinhood users).
- **Cursor acquisition:** [[spcx]] exercised an option to buy **Cursor** (~$4B revenue) for
  **$60B** (~15× sales; Chamath's math ≈ effectively ~$15B in stock terms). Backstory: Cursor
  ran on [[anthropic|Claude]], then Anthropic shipped a competing coding agent → Cursor built
  its own model on Elon's "Colossus" hardware.
- **[[elon-musk]]** = "world's first trillionaire" — [[david-sacks]] stresses it's **paper
  wealth** (1-yr lockup, not cash). Tesla/SpaceX **merger** floated; Elon swapped ~$300M Tesla
  options for shares.

## 3. Anthropic Fable 5 ban `[33:34]` — ⚠️ contested
- Commerce Sec **Howard Lutnik** ordered [[anthropic]] to restrict **Fable 5** to US citizens
  (days after a June 9 launch); unable/unwilling to geo-restrict that granularly, Anthropic
  **shut it down for everyone**.
- "**Mythos**" = the powerful model held 30 days for cyber testing; **Fable 5** = guardrailed
  public version. **Amazon** ([[andy-jassy]], an Anthropic investor + AWS host) flagged a
  **jailbreak**; [[dario-amodei]] called it narrow/not serious (and published a blog
  distinguishing jailbreak severity).
- Per **WaPo/Semafor** (as relayed by [[david-sacks]]): Anthropic expanded a "trusted partners"
  preview to ~50 firms without consulting the WH, including a group the WH deemed a China-linked
  national-security risk (**reported as SK Telecom**; Sacks declined to confirm). A Treasury-
  level call reportedly asked Dario to pull Fable; he declined until the export-control letter
  came.
- **Political subtext** (disputed): Reed Hoffman backing, Anthropic ↔ Trump-admin "beef," a
  Pete Hegseth ("Dept of War") tweet. Sacks denies political motivation; [[jason-calacanis]]
  pushes back that beef clearly exists but steelmans that identical steps from Gemini/OpenAI
  would've been approved.
- **Investment takeaway → [[ai-gatekeeping]]:** [[chamath-palihapitiya]] argues Frontier-Lab
  mistrust hands **hyperscalers** (AMZN/MSFT/GOOG) a gatekeeper/KYC moat (cartel/oligopoly);
  [[david-friedberg]] counters that market forces drive **open-source + stack fragmentation**
  (IBM-mainframe→disaggregation analogy).

## 5. Iran war MOU + market impact `[1:14:47]`
- Iran war (began Feb 28, 2026; ~110 days) → **MOU**, formal signing **June 19, Geneva**,
  mediated by Pakistan. Terms: 60-day ceasefire extension (+ Lebanon), **Strait of Hormuz
  reopening**, Iran gives up enriched uranium under IAEA supervision + freezes program 60 days,
  sanctions lifted + **$300B reconstruction** (Gulf-funded, not US), US drawdown.
- **Market read:** Chamath — "market's going to the moon"; Hormuz reopening → **oil flows**
  (risk-on). Unresolved: Israel sign-on, ballistic missiles, future enrichment.
