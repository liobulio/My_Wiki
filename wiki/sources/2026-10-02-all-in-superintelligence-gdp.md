# All-In 2026-10-02 — Super Intelligence Summit · GDP Beats · AI buildout

## TL;DR
本周五集。与本仓相关的两条信号：①宏观**偏强**——Sacks 引 Q2 GDP 上修至 2.2%、Atlanta Fed GDPNow ~3.7%、贫困率降至历史低位 10.2%（录制在 9 月非农公布前后，其引用的 "8 月非农 +162k" 为 9 月报告下修前旧值）；②**AI 建设 = 美国史上最大经济押注**、电网为 binding constraint——Sacks 引 WSJ"比铁路、运河、电网加起来还大"、"中国每十年翻一倍电网、美国 25 年基本持平"，批评"anti-Data Center witch hunt"；Friedberg 预言政府将把数据中心/GPU 当"公用事业"配额化监管。无任何 CEG/TLN/VST 或具体核电运营商点名。媒体源、非研究，观点为主持人个人（[[david-sacks]] 有白宫 AI-czar 利益冲突）。

## Key takeaways
- **[[david-sacks]]（GDP/经济强）**："Q2 GDP was revised up to 2.2% up from 1.5% … Economists had expected no revision."；"The Atlanta Fed GDP now is showing around 3.7% … August payrolls just came out plus 162,000. The consensus was only 55,000."；"The poverty rate in the U.S. has fallen to its lowest level in history, 10.2%."
- **[[david-sacks]]（AI 建设/电网）**："The Wall Street Journal said [the AI build-out] is bigger than the railroads, the canals, and the electric grid combined."；"China's doubling their grid every decade, and we've been flat for about 25 years."；20 世纪每十年翻倍电网、2000 年代起 flatline、"we have to get that back"，批"anti-Data Center witch hunt"。
- **[[david-friedberg]]（数据中心即公用事业）**："data centers becoming the utility, which is the clouds, the neoc clouds."——预言政府会按"a certain number of GPUs … inference … chips"配额化监管数据中心访问/用量（如"Y percent go to defense"）。
- 无 CEG/TLN/VST、无具体核电/IPP 点名。
