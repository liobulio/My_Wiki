# Bill Ackman — "What the Market is Missing"

[[bill-ackman]] (Pershing Square) interviewed at the All-In Liquidity Summit. Mostly a
philosophy + portfolio-construction conversation; one concrete actionable thesis ([[hhh]]).

## TL;DR
Ackman has shifted from trade-in/trade-out activism to **permanent, high-quality, hard-to-
disrupt holdings**. He thinks the "old-fashioned" mega-caps ([[amzn]], [[msft]], [[meta]]) are
**undervalued** while capital chases chips/energy — a 2000-style rotation. His flagship build is
turning **[[hhh|Howard Hughes]]** into a **[[insurance-float-compounding|Berkshire-style
insurance compounder]]**.

## Key takeaways
- `[01:27]` **Philosophy shift:** business *quality* — "long-term, durable, protected,
  non-disruptable growth" — is the dominant factor. Still activist, but "more on Twitter."
  Early model: Wendy's → spun off Tim Hortons (doubled money).
- `[04:41]` **AI = disruption risk up "enormously":** greatest era ever to build (unlimited
  compute/capital/talent) → incumbents more disruptable. Owns [[amzn]]/[[msft]]/[[meta]]; calls
  them **undervalued old-fashioned compounders** (analogy: 2000, when Berkshire hit its lowest
  valuation as money chased internet stocks). SaaS: worried about [[salesforce]]-type & niche
  high-priced software; per-seat platforms (MSFT ~$50/seat) safer.
- `[07:50]` **"Rubber-band effect":** valuation is a tether — too-cheap pulls prices back up.
  Cites his COVID-March-2020 CNBC call; recently called the market "way higher" because
  high-quality stocks got "crazy cheap."
- `[11:19]` **Underwrite AI/venture names like VC** (people·opportunity·context·deal):
  [[spcx|SpaceX]] "one of one," near-monopoly launch + Starlink; he's in an **xAI SPV** (via Ron
  Baron). Skeptical of 50–150× revenue. [[openai]]: capital commitments >> revenue = "hard."
- `[16:07]` **Founder-led advantage** → [[founder-led-companies]]: founders have authority +
  economic stake + reputation to make radical calls; avg S&P 500 CEO tenure ~3–4 yrs. Cites
  Zuckerberg (Instagram/WhatsApp).
- `[19:30]` **Berkshire 2.0 via [[hhh]]:** bought out of General Growth bankruptcy; "small
  cities" (Summerlin, 26k acres) like the Irvine Co. Trades at a discount to liquidation value
  (~$63/sh); pivoting cash into **insurance** to build a 50-yr compounding machine.
- `[26:39]` **Three ways to invest with Pershing:** (1) **Pershing Square Inc.** (the now-public
  manager — a fee "royalty"); (2) **[[psus]]** (best-ideas closed-end fund, ~18% discount to NAV,
  2% fee); (3) **[[hhh]]** (the Berkshire bet).

## Notable claims (checked below)
- Pershing track record: $1 (22 yrs ago) → ~27–28× net (~mid-40s× gross).
- [[hhh]] ~$63/sh, ~$4B mkt cap, discount to liquidation value.
- [[psus]] trading ~18% discount to NAV, 2% fee; London fund (PSH) ~30% discount.

## ✅ Fact-check (2026-06-26)
- **[[hhh|Howard Hughes]] → Berkshire 2.0 — ✅ verified:** Pershing raised its stake to ~**47%**,
  Ackman returned as exec chairman (May 2025); HHH is buying specialty insurer **Vantage Group
  for ~$2.1B** (~1.5× book, closing Q2 2026), with Pershing Square Holdings funding up to **$1B**
  via preferred. — [Yahoo/GuruFocus](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/bill-ackmans-next-berkshire-inside-170053965.html),
  [QuotedData](https://quoteddata.com/2025/12/pershing-square-holdings-puts-up-750m-to-back-howards-hughes-acquisition-of-insurer-vantage-group/)
- **[[psus]] / going public — ✅ verified, ⚠️ discount now deeper:** PSUS IPO'd **April 2026,
  $5B at $50**, closed debut **−18% at $40.90**; the **manager (Pershing Square Inc.)** listed
  alongside (combined IPO + placement). Ackman's "18% discount" = the debut; it has since
  **widened to ~24–29%** (London PSH ~30%). — [Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/118652/Bill-Ackmans-closed-end-fund-Pershing-Square-USA-prices-$),
  [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4897379-pershing-square-usa-expect-further-discounts-over-time)
- **Track record — ✅ consistent:** ~28× net over 22 yrs ≈ ~16.3% CAGR, in line with Pershing's
  reported ~16–17% since-2004 annualized.
- **⚠️ Projections, not facts:** "MSFT/META/AMZN are undervalued" and the rubber-band timing
  call are Ackman's *opinions*. Exact HHH price/mcap (~$63/~$4B) left ❓ source-reported.
- **Timeline note:** this was taped **before** the [[spcx|SpaceX]] IPO — Ackman floats
  "$750B–$1T," but the actual print (~June 11–12) was **~$1.77T** (see [[2026-06-20-all-in-oligarchs-fable-iran]]).
