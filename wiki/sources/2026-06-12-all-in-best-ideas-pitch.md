# All-In Best Ideas Pitch Competition (2026)

Four fund managers each pitch one top idea to the All-In besties, modeled on the old "Ira
Sohn" format. See the cross-cutting fact-check in [[all-in-best-ideas-2026-factcheck]].

## TL;DR
Four single-name pitches: [[mgm|MGM Resorts]] (event-driven gaming), [[tln|Talen Energy]]
(nuclear/AI power), [[akts|Aktis Oncology]] (radiopharma biotech), and [[geod|GEODNET]] (a
crypto/[[depin|DePIN]] RTK network). Independently fact-checked — three hold up well on the
facts; **GEODNET's headline revenue figures are ~2x overstated** vs reported data.

## The four pitches
1. `[02:34]` **[[aaron-cowen]]** (Suvretta Capital) → **[[mgm]]** — Vegas is fine, but the real
   value is hidden Asian/Osaka + Dubai optionality; [[barry-diller]] (26% owner) just bid
   $48 → "company in play," potential triple. Bull case: [[mgm-bull|see fact-check]].
2. `[13:09]` **[[dan-dreyfus]]** (Bornite Capital) → **[[tln]]** — buy a hard asset below
   replacement cost into a structural [[ai-power-demand|power shortage]]; ~7x FCF, AWS data
   center deal, optionality on building new capacity.
3. `[27:19]` **[[oleg-nodelman]]** (EcoR1 Capital) → **[[akts]]** — actinium-225
   [[radiopharmaceuticals|radiopharma]] miniprotein platform; nectin-4 + B7H3; Lilly-backed
   IPO; data catalysts in 2027.
4. `[40:20]` **[[kyle-samani]]** (Multicoin Capital) → **[[geod]]** — world's largest RTK
   network, decentralized buildout, 80% of revenue buys back the token; physical-AI tailwind.

## Results
- **Audience vote** (150 votes): 1) Dreyfus/Talen 50% · 2) Cowen/MGM 24% · 3) Nodelman/Aktis
  21% · 4) Samani/GEODNET 5%.
- **Besties vote:** 1) Cowen/MGM · 2) Dreyfus/Talen · 3) Nodelman/Aktis · 4) Samani/GEODNET.
  → **"MGM wins."**
- Besties' framing: MGM & Talen = sizeable, downside-protected ("people will always gamble
  and leave the lights on"); Aktis & GEODNET = "lottery tickets" (high upside, real chance of
  zero). Chamath claimed he put $200k into MGM and Talen in real time; GEODNET too illiquid to
  size (couldn't get $1M in).

## Notable claims (checked separately)
- [[mgm]]: Diller owns 26%, bid $48; Osaka casino opens 2030; Vegas+China ≈ low-$60s; "triple."
- [[tln]]: stock "high $300s" (transcript also says "$30s" — error); ~$50/sh FCF doing nothing
  → 7x; replacement cost $45B vs $25B EV.
- [[akts]]: $1B mkt cap / $500M EV; $300M IPO 18x oversubscribed; Lilly $100M; data 2027.
- [[geod]]: 22,000 nodes; ~$11M revenue, $8.8M/yr token buybacks; ~$150M FDV.
