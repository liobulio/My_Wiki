# FERC — Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

## Who
The U.S. **Federal Energy Regulatory Commission** regulates interstate transmission and
wholesale sale of electricity and natural gas, and oversees the RTO/ISO capacity markets
(including **PJM**) that set the price signal for firm generation. Its rulings on
co-location, interconnection service agreements (ISAs), uprates and capacity-market design
directly bear on the [[ai-power-ipp-bull]] thesis — **Invalidation #2** names FERC explicitly
("uprate / co-location agreements restricted by regulators"). Precedent in the wiki: FERC's
2024-11-01 rejection of the amended Susquehanna–AWS ISA ([[tln]]).

## House view (current)
- **2026-09-29** — FERC **accepted but suspended PJM's Reliability Backstop Procurement for
  five months** (effective 2027-02-28, subject to refund + paper hearing), prompting PJM to
  **pull its Sept 30 backstop auction** (~6,831 MW target). FERC found three parts "may be
  unjust and unreasonable": **cost allocation** (PJM "did not adequately ensure that the
  procurement's costs will be allocated to the customers who drive its need"), transmission-
  owner exit rules, and LSE collateral requirements. Chair **Laura Swett**: *"This commission
  will not be forced into accepting a deeply flawed, eleventh-hour procurement mechanism with
  billion-dollar implications for consumers."* A **PJM capacity-market intervention** in the
  Invalidation #2 channel of [[ai-power-ipp-bull]] — but it suspends a reliability *backstop
  procurement* (a mechanism [[ceg]]/[[vst]] co-proposed), **not** any of our companies' own
  uprate/co-location agreements; co-location rules remain a separate pending proceeding
  (FERC order expected Q1–Q2 2027). [[2026-09-29-ferc-pjm-backstop-suspension]].

## Track record
