## What checked out vs what didn't

**[[mgm]] — ✅** Diller/People Inc. **$48.30/sh (~$18B)** bid, **26.1%** stake, **Osaka 2030
(40%)** all verified; an analyst even argues the bid *under-values* China+Osaka — supporting
Cowen's "don't tender." Dubai legalization and the $100–150 target remain speculative.

**[[tln]] — ✅** Price **~$382** (transcript's "$30s" is a typo for "high $300s"); **AWS deal**
(960 MW campus $650M + up to 1,920 MW Susquehanna PPA, ~$18B/2042) verified; ~2.2 GW nuclear
verified. Replacement-cost gap and $50/$70/$100 FCF ladder are Dreyfus's estimates; street
12-mo target ~$472.

**[[akts]] — ✅ (one caveat)** **$318M IPO** (≈ pitch's "$300M"), Ac-225/nectin-4 **AKY-1189**
with **FDA Fast Track**, all verified. **Caveat:** Lilly deal is **$60M upfront + up to $1.2B
milestones** (one outlet says "$100M stake") — the flat "$100M order" is imprecise. $10B/$200
is a projection.

**[[geod]] — ⚠️ The one to discount.** Network scale (~20,500 stations), 80% buyback-burn,
Solana migration, Multicoin backing, CEO Mike Horton — all verified. **But revenue is ~$5M
annualized, not ~$11M** (so buybacks ≈ ~$4M, not $8.8M), and the token is **net-inflationary
until ~2026–27**. Samani also disclosed a **conflict** (Multicoin led a round; he owns the
token) and contradicted himself on the revenue figure mid-pitch.

## Takeaways
- **Highest factual confidence:** MGM and Talen (also the besties' top two; sizeable,
  downside-protected).
- **Speculative "lottery tickets":** Aktis (binary biotech) and GEODNET (small, illiquid,
  inflated metrics). Aktis's facts are clean; **GEODNET's are not** — treat its headline
  numbers skeptically and re-derive from on-chain/Messari data before ever acting.
- **Pattern:** the single-name pitch with a **disclosed conflict of interest** ([[kyle-samani]]
  / [[geod]]) was also the one with the **overstated numbers**. Worth remembering as a filter.
