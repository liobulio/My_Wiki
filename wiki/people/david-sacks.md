# David Sacks

## Who
Craft Ventures founder; co-host of the [[all-in-podcast|All-In Podcast]]; **sitting White House
AI czar**. Long-time Elon associate (PayPal era), early [[spcx|SpaceX]] investor.

## ⚠️ Conflict of interest (read before weighting his claims)
On the [[anthropic]] Fable segment, Sacks is **simultaneously a government official describing a
government action, a podcast host, and a VC/Elon ally**. He says he "only got the readout once
the letter was sent" and named no one — but his account is **inherently conflicted** and
should be treated as an interested party's narrative, not neutral reporting.

self-serving regulatory capture. Defends the Fable action as a security reaction, not policy
or political beef (a claim [[jason-calacanis]] disputes on-air).
