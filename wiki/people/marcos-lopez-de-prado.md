# Marcos López de Prado

## Who
Professor of Practice at **Cornell University** (Engineering); Global Head
of Quantitative Research & Development at **ADIA**; founder of **True
Positive Technologies (TPT)**. Earlier, head of quantitative trading and
research at Guggenheim Partners. Elected International Member of Spain's
Royal Academy of Engineering (2026).

## Views & style
Best known for rigor around **backtest overfitting** and financial machine
learning: author of *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* (Wiley, 2018)
and *Machine Learning for Asset Managers*. Key contributions include the
**Deflated Sharpe Ratio** (correcting Sharpe for selection bias and
non-normality), **meta-labeling**, and **purged/embargoed cross-validation**
for time-series ML in finance — all aimed at the same underlying discipline
this wiki's [[statarb-strategy-lab]] tries to enforce: don't trust a backtest
number until you've actively ruled out the ways it could be fooling you.
