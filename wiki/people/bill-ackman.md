# Bill Ackman

## Who
Founder/CEO of **Pershing Square**. Activist-turned-long-term concentrated investor; building a
**Berkshire-style** vehicle via [[hhh|Howard Hughes]]. Track record: ~28× net over 22 years
(~16% CAGR) — see [[2026-06-03-ackman-market-is-missing]].

## Views & style
- **Quality over activism:** the dominant factor is "long-term, durable, protected,
  **non-disruptable** growth." Still activist, but increasingly via Twitter, not proxy fights.
- **AI = disruption risk up "enormously":** spends most time assessing destructibility. Owns
  [[amzn]]/[[msft]]/[[meta]] and calls them **undervalued** "old-fashioned compounders" while
  capital chases chips/energy (2000-rotation analogy).
- **Underwrite venture/AI names like VC** (people·opportunity·context·deal) — long [[spcx]] /
  xAI thesis; skeptical of 50–150× revenue; flags [[openai]] capital-commitments-vs-revenue.
- **[[founder-led-companies|Founder-led advantage]]** and **[[insurance-float-compounding]]**
  are his two big structural lenses.

## Notable calls
- COVID **March 2020**: public CNBC call (hedged via CDS, then bought equities) — famous win.
- **General Growth Properties:** bought near-bankruptcy (~$0.34 → ~$34); [[hhh|Howard Hughes]]
  spun out of it. Early: **Wendy's → Tim Hortons** spin-off.
- ⚠️ Pre-IPO he pegged [[spcx|SpaceX]] at "$750B–$1T"; the actual IPO printed ~$1.77T.
