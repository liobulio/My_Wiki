# Dario Amodei

## Who
Co-founder & CEO of [[anthropic]] (maker of Claude). Author of long-form essays on AI
(referenced: "Machines of Loving Grace"). A leading proponent of AI-safety-first development.

## Views & style (as characterized in the source)
Per the [[2026-06-20-all-in-oligarchs-fable-iran|episode]], advocates that advanced AI is
dangerous and warrants government + a small set of trusted labs controlling it — which the
hosts (esp. [[david-sacks]]) frame critically as centralization/"cartel." Reportedly called the
Fable jailbreak "narrow" and published a blog distinguishing jailbreak severity.

> ("epistemic exceptionalism"). That is **entertainment/opinion about a real person — not fact**
> and is **not** recorded here as biographical truth.
