# Ryan Cohen

## Who
Founder of [[chewy]] (sold 2017, ~$3.35B); chairman/CEO of [[gme|GameStop]]; activist investor
(RC Ventures). Now bidding **$56B for [[ebay]]**. Archetypal [[founder-led-companies|founder-
operator]].

## Views & style
- **Founder-operator > professional management:** own-money skin-in-the-game, roll up sleeves,
  solve root causes; contemptuous of "overpaid, no-skin" boards/management that "hire
  consultants." Declined a ~$35B GameStop pay package to focus on the eBay bid.
- **Operating playbook:** supply-chain/cost obsession ("game of pennies"), "**will over
  skill**" hiring, negative working capital, focus over diversification.
- **Investing:** buys **out-of-favor, hated, established** businesses (running "into a burning
  house"); went from passive to [[activist-investing|activist]] (13G→13D) at GameStop.
- ⚠️ Associated with the **meme-stock / "vibes valuation"** phenomenon — [[bill-ackman]] cited
  GameStop as the example ([[2026-06-03-ackman-market-is-missing]]); Cohen counters with Graham's
  "voting machine short-term, weighing machine long-term."

## Notable calls
- **Chewy** — built vs Amazon, sold ~$3.35B (2017); IPO'd far higher later.
- **GameStop** — activist 2020–21, board then CEO; cost-cut + pivot to **collectibles** (~42% of
  Q1'26 revenue); $9.7B cash, record profit, $2B buyback.
- **eBay** — $56B ($125/sh, 50/50) bid; **rejected** May 2026; vows to persist.
