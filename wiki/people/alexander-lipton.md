# Alexander Lipton

## Who
Quantitative finance researcher; Global Head of Research & Development at
**ADIA** (Abu Dhabi Investment Authority) and advisory-board member of ADIA
Lab; MIT Connection Science Fellow; Dean's Fellow / visiting professor at
Hebrew University of Jerusalem's business school. Earlier career included
Global Head of Quantitative Research at Bank of America Merrill Lynch and a
managing-director role at Citadel.

## Views & style
Known for FX and derivatives modeling, and for applying the **method of heat
potentials** — a technique borrowed from heat-conduction physics — to
mathematical finance problems (barrier option pricing, first-passage-time
problems for mean-reverting processes). Also active in fintech/stablecoin
and CBDC research.

## Notable calls
- Co-author (with [[marcos-lopez-de-prado]]) of
  [[2020-02-08-lipton-lopez-de-prado-ou-exit-rules|"A Closed-Form Solution
  for Optimal Mean-Reverting Trading Strategies"]] (2020) — see
  [[ou-optimal-exit-rules]]. The pair jointly won Risk.net's 2021 Buy-Side
  Quant of the Year award, largely for this work.
