# Brad Gerstner

## Who
Founder & CEO of **Altimeter Capital**, a tech-focused growth/crossover fund. Long-time
public+private AI/software investor; recurring "fifth bestie" guest on the [[all-in-podcast]],
where he delivers a periodic "market check / state of the market" keynote. Interviews Jensen
Huang and Sam Altman; large [[nvda|Nvidia]] holder. Known outside markets for the "Invest
America"/Trump-accounts child-savings push and the Center for Heart Attack Prevention.

## Views & style
- **Data-driven bull, not a permabull.** Frames the AI market as an **earnings-driven** (not
  multiple-expansion) super-cycle, and insists 2026 is "not 2000" — [[nvda]] at ~14× next
  year's fully-taxed GAAP earnings, NASDAQ/S&P/SOX all **below** their average multiples.
- **The offtake-revenue test.** His single most-watched datapoint is the **monthly revenue of
  the top AI labs** (Anthropic/OpenAI/xAI): the ~$1.5T/yr hyperscaler capex only works if
  offtake revenue climbs from ~$200B run-rate → $450B → $800B–$1T. "Follow the facts."
- **Power is a top-3 risk.** Names regulation, **power**, and interest rates as the three
  challenges; "power equipment is sold out", grid-interconnection delays, permitting/local
  opposition make the 43 GW 2027 build "too aggressive" (he pegs ~25 GW). Directly on-thesis
  for [[ai-power-ipp-bull]].
- **Position discipline:** currently "medium, mentally flexible" — will add on strong lab
  revenue + falling oil, trim if rates/regulation bite. "Everybody knows about AI. It's all
  priced. Now it's about facts and circumstances. Don't Yolo."

## Notable calls
- Oct-2025 pod with Sam Altman: pressed him on "$1T capex vs $13B GAAP revenue" — Altman told
  him to sell his shares; Gerstner argues the revenue then "showed up" (Anthropic run-rate
  $2B→$4B→$11B Jan–Mar 2026). Outcome: NASDAQ +15% YTD 2026 by mid-Sept.
