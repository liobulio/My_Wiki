# Alex Karp

## Who
Co-founder/CEO of [[pltr|Palantir]]. Idiosyncratic, combative public communicator. Leading voice
for **[[ai-sovereignty]]** — enterprises/governments owning their compute, models, data, and
"alpha" rather than renting from frontier labs.

## Views & style
- The frontier labs ([[anthropic]], [[openai]]) have caused enterprise **"loss of trust"**; you
  shouldn't hand your IP to a provider that will vertically integrate and compete with you.
- "AI safety" *for an enterprise* = **control** over your compute/models/data stack — not the
  frontier-lab framing that open models are "dangerous."
- "Are we really going to outsource the battlefield to the consensus view in Silicon Valley?"
